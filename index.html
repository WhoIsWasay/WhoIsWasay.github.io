01 AI × formal methods

Gödel

Formal verification for smart contracts, without the PhD.

Audit results ↗

Gödel reads Solidity, proposes invariants, and proves or disproves them with Z3. A supervisor critiques the candidates, a specifier turns them into formal properties, and a Foundry gatekeeper replays every counterexample on a live EVM. The output is a proof — not a probability score.

04 — About

Proof over probability.

I work at the intersection of AI, formal methods, and financial infrastructure. My default question is not “does it run?” but “can I prove it does what it claims?”

That question led to Gödel: an attempt to make formal verification fast enough to sit inside a normal engineering workflow. Before that, it led to Sprout: a payments platform where the ledger, the idempotency, and the failure modes have to be right the first time.

  • 01

    If it can't be verified, it isn't finished. Tests are evidence. Proofs are stronger.

  • 02

    Boring infrastructure, carefully built. The best systems are the ones nobody has to think about.

  • 03

    Ship the whole system. The API, the ledger, the UI, the docs — not just the demo.
